## Not run — why the gate is inadmissible

The standard preset's remaining surfaces were **not** exercised: the full 154-script
tx-zoo, the 99-script bidirectional parity matrix across both sockets,
`09-cli-parity`, adversarial N2N, the UTxO RPC suite, chaos, the
gov-enactment/rewards/kes/rollback/hardfork rounds, and 4-round tip-parity
sampling.

Consequence, stated plainly: **this release has no bidirectional parity evidence
and no cli-parity evidence.** #1058's PV11 tag change and #1026's GOV tag-12 arm
are covered by byte-golden unit tests but were not exercised against a live
cardano-node on the wire.

## #1057 — found, diagnosed, attempted, reverted

Not fixed in this release, and the release notes say so. The two-layer
genesis-anchor fix passed four RED-proven unit tests and was then found wrong on a
live devnet: storage can emit a genesis-rooted `SwitchPlan`, but the ledger cannot
execute one — rolling back to Origin aborts, leaving dugite-bp at block 4 while
the network was at 84. The wedge **moves** rather than going away.

Post-revert verification on a fresh two-forger devnet with the rebuilt binary:
dugite-bp block 45 / relay 46 / arbiter 46, 32 blocks forged, and **0** occurrences
of each of `ChainDB fork divergence`, `Fork rollback failed`,
`intersection=00000000`, `Rollback target outside LedgerSeq`.

The durable lesson, recorded on #1057 and in CLAUDE.md: **a RED-proven unit test
bounds the function, not the system.**
